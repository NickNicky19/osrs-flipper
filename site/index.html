05 / EVIDENCE

Method & source health

What the numbers mean

Maximum buy and target sell use the latest reported instant-sell and instant-buy transactions. A buy offer at the lower reference and a later sell offer at the higher reference are hypothetical. Conditional GP = assumed quantity × (target sell − per-unit GE tax − maximum buy).

Quantity is capped by public buy limit, 10% of each reported hourly side volume, available shared paper cash and a 125m GP position cap. The 10% rule is a stated stress assumption, not an estimate of order depth or your remaining allowance.

Exact units traded at a displayed average price and the quantity our offer might fill are unknown. Losses, unfilled windows and residual inventory are not silently counted as wins.

Source & freshness

Feed: OSRS Wiki real-time prices API, OSRS main-game routes. Guide prices are separate context. GitHub schedules may be delayed or dropped; this browser expires plans using their own timestamps.

Learning state

The fixed CPU candidate forecasts the next reported 5m lower-side average and units. Forecasts do not predict fills. The rules baseline remains active unless predeclared chronological, later shadow and matched-portfolio gates all pass. No private execution records are used here.

Evidence classes

  • Conditional scenario: arithmetic if the stated trades complete.
  • Historical simulation: a retrospective rule replay under named assumptions; none is displayed as real profit.
  • Forward paper: preissued plans with later public observations, still no verified fills.
  • Actual user recorded: private and absent from this website.

The 50m GP live-pilot ceiling is provisional and separate from this 500m GP paper account. All in-game actions are manual.
